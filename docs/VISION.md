# Vision

Practice the **same GitOps topology** as PML `pml-argocd` (tenant folder, argocd-deployable roots, bootstrap Helm, nested app-of-apps) on Kind — without multi-repo product delivery or a full infra stack (Istio, Kafka, observability).

One tenant (`platform/`), two clusters, three demo apps. Scale the *pattern*, not the platform surface area.
